A useful build answers four questions

These are the same questions we use across build-oriented pages.

What is the role?

Define what the setup must accomplish before choosing equipment.

What are the requirements?

Identify stats, resources or slots that must be committed first.

What is the trade-off?

Understand what the setup gives up in exchange for its strength.

What is the upgrade path?

Know which improvement matters now and which can wait.
