Baldur's Gate 3 Exploration Planning Guide

Plan Baldur's Gate 3 exploration around dialogue, utility, alternate routes and party coverage with minimal spoilers.

Bring capability coverage

Exploration is smoother when the party can handle dialogue, locks, traps, movement and environmental problems.

Check alternate routes

Many areas reward looking for height, side paths or non-combat solutions before committing to the obvious route.

Separate discovery from completion

You do not need to finish every encounter immediately. Mark or remember unresolved areas and return with a better plan.

Save resources for uncertainty

Exploration is harder when the party reaches an unknown fight already depleted.
