Baldur's Gate 3 New Player Checklist

A spoiler-light Baldur's Gate 3 new player checklist covering party setup, resources, exploration and combat habits.

Read what your abilities actually do

Many early mistakes come from assuming an action works like a similar ability in another game. Check range, resource cost, concentration and conditions.

Use the environment

Height, surfaces, line of sight and positioning can change a fight. Do not treat every encounter as a flat damage race.

Rest when it supports your resource economy

Spell slots and limited abilities are part of party planning. Hoarding every resource can make ordinary encounters harder than necessary.

Explore with purpose

Talk to characters, inspect paths and pay attention to alternative routes. Many problems have more than one solution.

Save before major experiments

Trying unusual approaches is part of the game. A sensible save routine lets you experiment without turning every mistake into a long replay.
