Baldur's Gate 3 Exploration Skills Overview

A Baldur's Gate 3 exploration reference for understanding dialogue, locks, traps, perception, movement and utility coverage.

Exploration is a party capability

Combat strength is only one part of party design. Exploration skills determine how many non-combat problems the group can solve without changing plans.

CapabilityWhy it matters
DialogueCreates options in conversations and conflict resolution.
Locks and trapsOpen routes, protect resources and reveal alternatives.
PerceptionHelps notice hidden dangers, objects or opportunities.
MovementExpands access to alternate paths and positions.
UtilitySolves environmental or situational problems outside direct combat.
