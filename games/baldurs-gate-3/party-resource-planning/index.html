Baldur's Gate 3 Party Resource Planning Guide

Plan Baldur's Gate 3 party resources across encounters by balancing limited abilities, recovery and encounter difficulty.

Spend resources where they change the fight

Powerful actions matter most when they prevent damage, remove a dangerous threat or create a decisive advantage.

Track the party, not one character

A party may still be ready even if one character is low on resources, or exhausted even when another is nearly full.

Use recovery deliberately

Resting is part of the resource economy, not a failure of efficiency.

Keep a reserve for uncertainty

Unknown encounters reward parties that avoid spending every strong option on routine fights.
