Baldur's Gate 3 Combat Preparation Guide

Prepare for Baldur's Gate 3 combat by checking positioning, resources, party roles and likely encounter constraints.

Start from position

Before committing, look at elevation, choke points, cover, hazards and where enemies can reach your party.

Know who acts first

Your opening actions should establish control or remove a major threat. Avoid wasting early turns on low-impact movement when better positioning was possible beforehand.

Check limited resources

Powerful abilities are valuable, but not every fight deserves the same resource spend. Match the investment to the encounter.

Protect concentration and key roles

If the plan depends on one effect or one character, position and support them accordingly.

Have a fallback

Good preparation includes a response for failed control, missed attacks or an unexpected enemy. Flexible parties recover better when the first plan breaks.
