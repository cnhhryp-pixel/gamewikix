Baldur's Gate 3 Party Roles Overview

A Baldur's Gate 3 party roles reference for understanding damage, control, support, sustain and exploration coverage.

Party roles describe capabilities

A useful party does not need one exact class combination. What matters is whether the group can solve the important combat and exploration problems it encounters.

RoleMain contribution
DamageRemove priority threats efficiently.
ControlLimit enemy movement, actions or access to important areas.
SupportImprove allies through buffs, setup or utility.
SustainHelp the party recover or survive pressure.
ExplorationHandle dialogue, locks, traps, movement or environmental problems.
