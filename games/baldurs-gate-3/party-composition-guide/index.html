Baldur's Gate 3 Party Composition Guide

A Baldur's Gate 3 party composition framework based on combat roles, control, support and exploration coverage.

Think in capabilities, not fixed classes

A strong party covers important jobs without requiring one exact class combination. Different classes can solve the same problem in different ways.

Cover damage and control

Reliable damage ends fights, while control prevents enemies from acting effectively. A party that can do both has more options when encounters become messy.

Add sustain and support

Healing, buffs, protection and recovery improve consistency. Support is strongest when it also contributes useful actions outside emergencies.

Plan for exploration

Dialogue, locks, traps, mobility and utility spells can matter as much as combat. Consider who handles these tasks before entering a difficult area.

Avoid role duplication without a reason

Two characters can share a role, but make sure that overlap provides a benefit rather than leaving another important capability uncovered.
