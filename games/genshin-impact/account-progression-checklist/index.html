Genshin Impact Account Progression Checklist

A practical Genshin Impact account progression checklist for building a stable roster without spreading resources too thin.

Stabilize your main team

Make sure your primary team can handle the content you are currently trying to clear before expanding aggressively.

Develop a second option gradually

A second team or alternate damage profile becomes useful once your first group is reliable. Build it in stages rather than splitting every resource equally from day one.

Maintain weapons and supports

Strong carries still depend on usable support characters and appropriate equipment. Do not treat support investment as optional.

Keep a resource reserve

Leaving some flexible resources unspent makes it easier to react to a new character, weapon or team idea without stopping progress elsewhere.

Review your roster periodically

As your account grows, old assumptions about the best team or most urgent upgrade can change.
