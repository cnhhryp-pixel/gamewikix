Genshin Impact Energy & Rotation Guide

A Genshin Impact guide to thinking about energy, burst uptime and repeatable rotations when building a team.

Energy is part of the rotation

A team that depends on bursts needs enough energy generation to repeat its intended sequence consistently.

Identify the energy-hungry characters

Not every team member needs the same amount of support. Focus on the characters whose burst availability controls the entire rotation.

Generate before you need it

Plan skill usage and swaps so particles and other energy sources arrive at the right point in the sequence.

Consistency beats one perfect rotation

A setup that works only after extra waiting may look strong in theory but feel awkward over repeated fights.
