Genshin Impact Team Roles Overview

A Genshin Impact team roles overview covering on-field damage, off-field damage, support, sustain and utility.

Roles describe what a character contributes

Role labels are useful when they explain the job a character performs in the team rather than forcing every character into one rigid category.

RoleMain contribution
On-field damageUses most active field time to drive the team’s main damage loop.
Off-field damageContinues contributing while another character is active.
Buffer / supportImproves teammates through buffs, debuffs or setup.
SustainProvides healing, shielding or other survival support.
UtilityImproves grouping, mobility, energy or rotation comfort.

Characters can cover multiple roles

Role compression is valuable because one slot can solve several team needs at once.
