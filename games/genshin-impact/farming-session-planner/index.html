Genshin Impact Farming Session Planner

Plan Genshin Impact farming sessions around one account bottleneck instead of spreading resin and time across unrelated goals.

Choose one account bottleneck

Pick the improvement that will unlock the most useful progress: level, weapon, talents, materials or another team member.

Prefer guaranteed progress first

Deterministic upgrades are easier to schedule than highly variable optimization.

Group compatible goals

If several characters share a useful resource path, a focused session can improve account-wide efficiency.

Stop when marginal value drops

Once the main bottleneck is fixed, re-evaluate instead of automatically continuing the same farm.
