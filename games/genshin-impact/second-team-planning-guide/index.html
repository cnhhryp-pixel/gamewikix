Genshin Impact Second Team Planning Guide

Plan a second Genshin Impact team without weakening your first team or spreading account resources too thin.

Stabilize the first team first

A second team is easier to build when your primary group already handles current content reliably.

Reuse account strengths

Look for weapons, supports or resource types that create value across both teams without causing direct competition.

Avoid duplicating the same bottleneck

If both teams depend on the same scarce role or resource, progression can stall.

Build the second team in layers

Start with the core interaction, then add support, sustain and optimization later.
