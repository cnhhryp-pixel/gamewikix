Genshin Impact Resource Priority Guide

A Genshin Impact resource priority guide for deciding what to farm first when building characters and teams.

Build one functional core first

Progress is smoother when a small number of characters are usable rather than many characters being partially built.

Fix the biggest bottleneck

Ask what is currently limiting performance: level, weapon, talent investment, artifacts, energy consistency or team support.

Prefer guaranteed upgrades early

Deterministic improvements are easier to plan than highly variable farming. Secure the upgrades you can control before chasing perfect optimization.

Stop when the return becomes small

Once a character is functional, compare the value of further optimization with improving another team member who is still weak.

Keep future teams in mind

Resources that work across several characters or teams can have better account-wide value than a highly specific investment.
