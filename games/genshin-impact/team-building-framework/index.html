Genshin Impact Team Building Framework

A practical Genshin Impact team building framework based on roles, reactions, energy, buffs and sustain.

Start with the team’s main job

Decide what the team is trying to do before choosing four characters. A clear damage plan makes later choices easier.

Assign roles

Think in terms of on-field damage, off-field damage, reaction support, buffs, healing or shielding. One character may cover several roles.

Check energy needs

A team that depends on bursts needs a reliable way to generate and funnel energy. Smooth rotations matter more than theoretical peak damage that cannot be repeated.

Use reactions intentionally

Do not add elements only for variety. Make sure they support the reaction pattern or utility the team actually wants.

Add sustain without breaking the plan

Healing or shielding should fit into the rotation cleanly. The best sustain option is one that keeps the team functional without forcing awkward downtime.
