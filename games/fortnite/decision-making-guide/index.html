Fortnite Decision-Making Guide

Improve Fortnite decision-making by evaluating information, position, resources and risk before committing to a fight.

Make decisions from four inputs

Before committing, check what you know, where you are, what resources you have and what happens if the fight lasts longer than expected.

Information

Do you know where the opponent is, whether another team is nearby, and what routes are still open?

Position

Good cover, elevation and a safe exit can make an average fight favorable. Poor position can make even strong mechanics hard to use.

Resources

Health, recovery, mobility and ammunition affect whether pressure is sustainable.

Risk

Ask what you gain by taking the fight now. Not every visible opponent needs to become an immediate engagement.
