Fortnite Inventory Management Guide

Organize a Fortnite inventory by combat roles, recovery and utility so each slot has a clear purpose.

Give every slot a job

An inventory is easier to use when each slot solves a different problem instead of holding several items that compete for the same role.

Cover the fight ranges you expect

Choose combat options that complement each other. Redundant weapons can reduce flexibility if healing, mobility or utility is missing.

Protect recovery capacity

Winning a fight at very low health can leave you vulnerable to the next opponent. Recovery is part of the loadout, not an afterthought.

Adjust to the match

Inventory decisions should change when your position, resources or expected engagements change. A rigid template is less useful than a role-based plan.
