Fortnite Fight Selection Guide

Improve Fortnite fight selection by evaluating position, resources, information and the value of committing.

Not every visible opponent is a good fight

Before committing, compare what you can gain with what you risk losing in position, health, time and resources.

Check four signals

  • Do you have reliable information?
  • Is your position defensible?
  • Can your loadout support the expected range?
  • Can you disengage if the fight gets worse?

Prefer fights with an advantage

Strong timing, cover or information can matter more than raw confidence.

Avoid chained risk

Weak health, weak position and low mobility together are a strong signal to reset instead of forcing another engagement.
