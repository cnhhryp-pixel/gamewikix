Fortnite Rotation Planning Guide

Plan Fortnite rotations around timing, terrain, cover, mobility and the risk of arriving late.

Rotate before urgency removes options

Early movement gives you more routes and more time to choose cover. Late movement often forces a predictable path.

Read terrain before moving

Open ground, elevation changes and chokepoints all affect how safe a route is.

Save mobility for real problems

Using mobility only for speed can leave you without an escape when pressure starts.

Arrive with resources intact

A good rotation should preserve enough health, mobility and attention for the next fight.
