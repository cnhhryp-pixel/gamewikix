Fortnite Mobility & Utility Overview

A Fortnite mobility and utility reference for understanding repositioning, escape options, information and situational tools.

Mobility solves position problems

Mobility is valuable because it changes where and when you can fight. It can help you escape weak situations, close distance or rotate safely.

Utility solves specific tactical problems

Utility is broader than damage. It can provide information, deny space, create openings or help a team control the pace of a fight.
