Fortnite Combat Roles Overview

A Fortnite combat roles reference for understanding close-range pressure, mid-range control, recovery, mobility and utility.

Think in combat roles

Fortnite loadouts are easier to understand when each slot has a job rather than being treated as a fixed list of item names.

RoleMain job
Close-range pressureHandle fights where reaction speed and burst pressure matter most.
Mid-range controlPressure opponents without immediately closing distance.
RecoveryRestore health or shields so one fight does not ruin the next.
MobilityReposition, escape weak fights or reach better terrain.
UtilityProvide information, space control or situational advantages.

Roles matter more than item names

The available loot pool can change, but the underlying jobs remain useful for planning flexible loadouts.
