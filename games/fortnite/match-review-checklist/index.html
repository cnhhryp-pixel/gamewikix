Fortnite Match Review Checklist

A concise Fortnite match review checklist for learning from positioning, fighting, rotations and resource mistakes.

Review the turning point

You do not need to analyze every second. Find the moment when the match became significantly harder and start there.

Ask five questions

  • Did I have useful information?
  • Was my position defensible?
  • Did my loadout cover the fight I took?
  • Was I healthy and resourced enough to commit?
  • Could I have disengaged earlier?

Separate mechanics from decisions

A missed shot and a bad push are different problems. Practice mechanics separately; fix decision patterns by recognizing the situation earlier.

Choose one correction

End each review with one behavior to test next match. Too many simultaneous corrections are difficult to remember under pressure.
