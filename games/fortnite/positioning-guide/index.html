Fortnite Positioning Guide

Improve Fortnite positioning by thinking about cover, angles, escape routes and when a fight is actually worth taking.

Position before you shoot

Many fights are decided before the first shot. Ask whether you have cover, a usable angle and somewhere to move if the fight turns against you.

Avoid fighting from open space

When possible, move from cover to cover instead of stopping in exposed areas. Better cover gives you more time to heal, reload and choose the next action.

Know your escape route

A strong position is not only good for attacking. It should also give you a way to disengage, rotate or change elevation.

Do not stack bad decisions

Weak position plus low resources plus poor information is usually a sign to reset rather than force the fight.

Review where damage starts

After a match, notice where you first lost control. Often the useful lesson is the position that caused the damage, not the final elimination.
