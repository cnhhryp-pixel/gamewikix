Roblox Progression Systems Overview

A Roblox progression systems reference for understanding loops, unlocks, quests, upgrades and long-term account growth.

Progression systems answer one question

What changes after you repeat the main activity? Most progression-focused experiences combine several systems that turn repeated play into stronger options or new access.

SystemMain purpose
Core loopThe repeatable activity that creates progress.
UnlocksOpen new areas, tools, features or difficulty tiers.
UpgradesImprove efficiency, power, capacity or convenience.
QuestsProvide structured goals and often teach the intended path.
Long-term progressionCreates goals that persist across many sessions.
