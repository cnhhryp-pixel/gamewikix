Roblox Upgrade Systems Overview

A Roblox upgrade systems reference for understanding power, capacity, speed, access and quality-of-life improvements.

Upgrades remove bottlenecks

The most useful way to compare upgrades is to ask which part of the main gameplay loop they improve.

Upgrade typeWhat it changes
PowerImproves damage, production or output.
CapacityLets you hold, carry or process more before resetting.
SpeedReduces time spent on repeated movement or actions.
AccessUnlocks areas, systems or higher progression tiers.
Quality of lifeReduces repeated friction without directly increasing power.
