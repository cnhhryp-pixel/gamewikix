RIVALS Beginner Guide

A RIVALS beginner guide for Roblox players learning FPS duels, cover usage, movement and round-by-round decision-making.

Start with clean duels

Your first goal is not complicated movement or flashy plays. Learn to enter fights with your crosshair ready, use cover, and avoid exposing yourself to multiple angles at once.

Use cover as a reset tool

Cover is valuable because it gives you time to reload, reposition and break the opponent's line of sight. Do not leave cover without a reason.

Move without destroying your aim

Movement should make you harder to hit while still allowing consistent shots. If movement is causing most of your misses, simplify it until your accuracy improves.

Learn weapon roles

Think in ranges and jobs. A weapon that is comfortable in one distance may be weak in another. Adjust your position before forcing the weapon into a bad fight.

Review one lost round

Ask whether the loss came from aim, position, timing or information. Pick one correction for the next round instead of changing everything at once.
