RIVALS Aim & Duel Practice Guide

A structured RIVALS practice guide for improving first-shot accuracy, movement, peeking, duel review and repeatable FPS habits.

Practice one failure mode at a time

If you miss first shots, train crosshair placement. If you die while re-peeking, train timing and cover discipline. If movement ruins accuracy, simplify movement until aim is stable.

Block 1: first-shot quality

Focus on having the crosshair ready before the opponent enters the angle. Do not rely on large emergency flicks as the default.

Block 2: peek discipline

Expose as little as necessary, take the shot, then reset. Re-peeking the same angle repeatedly makes you predictable.

Block 3: movement under pressure

Use movement to change the opponent's shot difficulty without turning your own aim into chaos.

Block 4: short review

After a small set of matches, identify the single most repeated mistake and make it the next session's focus.
