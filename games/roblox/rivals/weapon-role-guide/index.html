RIVALS Weapon Role Guide

Think about RIVALS weapons by combat role, range and consistency instead of chasing a single universal best weapon.

Weapons should solve a range problem

Instead of asking which weapon is best overall, ask what range and fight type your current setup handles poorly.

RoleMain job
Close-range pressureWin short, high-speed engagements.
Mid-range consistencyMaintain reliable pressure without overcommitting.
Long-range controlPunish exposure and force careful movement.
Utility / flexibilityCover unusual situations or support another weapon role.

Build around comfort and repeatability

A theoretically strong weapon is less useful if its timing, range or recoil pattern does not fit how you actually take fights.
