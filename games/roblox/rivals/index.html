RIVALS Wiki & Beginner Guides

RIVALS beginner help for duel fundamentals, practice structure, weapon-role thinking and improving decision-making in Roblox FPS matches.

What RIVALS is about

RIVALS is built around competitive FPS duels, ranging from 1v1 to team formats. The core improvement loop is simple: take repeated fights, identify why rounds are lost, and train the specific weakness instead of only playing more matches.

What to learn first

  • Crosshair placement and clean first shots.
  • Movement that keeps you hard to hit without ruining your own aim.
  • Using cover before committing to a duel.
  • Understanding the role of each weapon instead of treating every fight the same.
Weapons, maps and balance can change. This mini-wiki focuses on FPS fundamentals that remain useful across updates.
