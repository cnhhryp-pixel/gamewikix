RIVALS Positioning Guide

Improve Roblox RIVALS positioning with better cover use, angle choice, spacing and escape routes in duels and team fights.

Good aim is easier from a good position

Positioning changes how difficult a duel is before the first shot. Cover, spacing and angle choice can reduce the amount of mechanical perfection you need.

Use cover as a reset point

Cover should give you somewhere to break line of sight, reload, recover and choose the next peek instead of forcing a continuous aim duel.

Limit simultaneous threats

In team fights, avoid standing where multiple opponents can see you at once. A strong angle exposes one target while protecting you from others.

Do not corner yourself

A position is weak if it gives you no way to disengage after missing your first shots.
