99 Nights in the Forest Camp Planning Guide

Plan a more efficient 99 Nights in the Forest camp by separating storage, gathering, exploration and team responsibilities.

Give the camp clear jobs

A useful camp is easier to operate when supplies, recovery and departure preparation are organized around repeated tasks.

Create a ready-to-leave zone

Keep common supplies for exploration together so players do not spend the start of every trip searching through camp.

Separate bulk resources from critical supplies

Frequently needed survival items should be easier to find than large quantities of low-priority material.

Use team roles deliberately

Assign temporary jobs such as gathering, camp maintenance and exploration. Roles can rotate, but each trip should still have a clear division of responsibility.

Review the camp after a failed run

If players repeatedly run out of the same resource or waste time preparing, treat that as a camp-design problem and fix the workflow.
