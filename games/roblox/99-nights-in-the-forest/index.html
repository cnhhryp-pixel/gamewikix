99 Nights in the Forest Wiki & Beginner Guides

99 Nights in the Forest beginner help for camp planning, team roles, resource priorities and structured survival sessions.

What the experience is about

The official experience description centers on building a camp with friends while surviving an ominous forest setting. This mini-wiki focuses on the stable survival ideas behind that loop: camp organization, resource priorities, role coverage and preparing before moving farther from safety.

What to learn first

  • Keep a clear safe return point.
  • Separate gathering, camp work and exploration tasks.
  • Carry enough supplies to return instead of maximizing every trip.
  • Use team roles so multiple players are not duplicating the same task.
Updates may add or change classes, items or systems. The pages here intentionally focus on evergreen survival planning.
