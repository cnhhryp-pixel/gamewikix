99 Nights in the Forest Team Role Guide

Organize a 99 Nights in the Forest team around gathering, camp work, exploration and flexible support roles.

Roles reduce duplicated work

A team becomes more efficient when players know who is gathering, who is maintaining camp stability, and who is extending exploration.

RoleMain responsibility
GathererBring back the resources currently blocking progress.
Camp supportKeep the safe area organized and ready for the next trip.
ExplorerScout routes and objectives without risking the whole team.
FlexMove to whichever role is currently overloaded.

Roles should change with the session

Do not treat assignments as permanent classes. Reassign players when camp needs or exploration goals change.
