99 Nights in the Forest Resource Priorities

Prioritize resources in 99 Nights in the Forest by separating immediate survival, camp stability and longer-term progression needs.

Collect for the next problem

Resource gathering is most efficient when each trip answers a specific camp or survival need.

PriorityPurpose
Immediate survivalKeep the team able to return, recover and continue the session.
Camp stabilitySupport the safe base loop and repeated preparation.
ExpansionEnable longer trips or more ambitious objectives.
Optional upgradesImprove efficiency after core survival needs are stable.

Avoid mixed-purpose trips

Trips become inefficient when every player collects random items without knowing what the camp needs next.
