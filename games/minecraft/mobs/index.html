Minecraft Mobs Overview

A Minecraft mobs overview organized by behavior, danger, drops and how players interact with them.

Think in behavior groups

Mobs are easier to learn when grouped by how they interact with the player rather than as one alphabetical list.

GroupTypical interaction
PassiveGenerally do not attack the player and may provide food, materials or utility.
NeutralUsually become dangerous only under certain conditions or after provocation.
HostileActively threaten the player and require combat, avoidance or defensive planning.
Utility / specialHave unusual roles in trading, transport, defense or world systems.

Learn the danger trigger

For unfamiliar mobs, the most useful questions are: when does it attack, what range does it threaten, and what environment makes the encounter harder?
