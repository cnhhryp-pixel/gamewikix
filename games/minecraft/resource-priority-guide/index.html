Minecraft Resource Priority Guide

A simple Minecraft resource priority guide for deciding what to gather first and what can wait in a new survival world.

Prioritize resources by the problem they solve

The best early resource is not always the rarest one. A better rule is to collect resources that remove your current bottleneck.

Immediate priorities

  • Wood: basic crafting, tools and building.
  • Food: keeps exploration and combat sustainable.
  • Stone-level materials: improve basic tools and reduce collection time.
  • Fuel: supports cooking and processing.

Next-step priorities

Once basic survival is stable, focus on materials that improve efficiency, safety and storage. That usually means better tools, stronger protection, lighting, reliable transport and organized inventory space.

Do not over-collect too early

A common trap is filling storage with huge amounts of low-priority blocks while still lacking food, spare tools or a safe route home. Gather bulk materials when you have a project that needs them.

Use project-based gathering

Before leaving your base, define the goal: “I need enough material for a storage room” is better than “I will collect everything I see.” Project-based gathering makes each trip more efficient and easier to evaluate.
