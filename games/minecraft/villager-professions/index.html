Minecraft Villager Professions Overview

A Minecraft villager professions reference for understanding job roles, trade categories and how professions fit into a long-term trading setup.

Professions define trade categories

Villager professions matter because they determine the type of resources a villager can exchange and the role that villager plays in your trading system.

Profession planning questionWhy it matters
What does this profession trade?It determines whether the villager solves a repeated need.
Can you supply the input?Renewable or easy-to-gather inputs make the trade easier to sustain.
Does the profession duplicate another villager?Too much overlap can waste space and management effort.
Where does it fit in the base?Organization makes repeated trading faster and safer.

Build the profession mix around your world

The best profession mix depends on what you build, gather and replace often. A useful trading hall reflects your own resource loop rather than a fixed universal template.
