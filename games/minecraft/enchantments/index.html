Minecraft Enchantments Overview

A practical overview of Minecraft enchantment categories and how to think about tool, weapon, armor and utility enchantments.

What enchantments do

Enchantments modify how equipment performs. The useful way to organize them is by the job they improve rather than memorizing one giant list.

CategoryMain purpose
Tool enchantmentsImprove gathering speed, durability or how blocks are collected.
Weapon enchantmentsImprove damage, crowd control or situational combat utility.
Armor enchantmentsImprove protection, movement or survival in specific environments.
Utility enchantmentsReduce maintenance, increase longevity or add quality-of-life benefits.

Build around the item’s job

Choose enchantments based on what the item is for. A mining tool, travel item and combat weapon have different priorities.

Avoid conflicting goals

When two upgrades compete for the same slot or purpose, choose the one that supports the activity you perform most often.
