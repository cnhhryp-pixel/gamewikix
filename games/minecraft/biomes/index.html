Minecraft Biomes Overview

A Minecraft biome reference organized around terrain, resources, travel and base-planning considerations.

Biomes change more than scenery

Biome choice affects terrain, nearby resources, travel difficulty, available building materials and how convenient a location is for a long-term base.

Biome factorWhy it matters
TerrainChanges building cost, visibility and travel speed.
ResourcesDetermines which materials and natural features are easy to access nearby.
ClimateAffects visual style and some environmental behavior.
ConnectivityNearby oceans, rivers, mountains or open land can shape transport routes.

Choose by project

A scenic biome is not always the most convenient survival base. Pick a location that supports what you plan to build and how often you travel.
