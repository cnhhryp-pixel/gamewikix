Minecraft Armor Overview

A Minecraft armor reference focused on protection, durability, enchantment priorities and choosing equipment for the activity.

Armor is risk management

The value of armor depends on the kind of danger you expect and how costly a mistake would be.

ConsiderationPlanning question
ProtectionHow much damage margin do you need for the activity?
DurabilityWill the equipment survive the length of the trip?
EnchantmentsWhich upgrades match the repeated risks you face?
Replacement costHow painful would it be to lose this set?

Use better gear where risk justifies it

High-value gear is most useful when it protects something equally valuable: a difficult objective, long trip or expensive inventory.
