Elden Ring Stats Overview

An Elden Ring stats overview for understanding requirements, survivability, resources and offensive scaling without forcing one build.

Stats are build decisions

Attributes matter because they unlock equipment, improve survivability, support resources or strengthen the way a build deals damage.

Stat purposeQuestions to ask
RequirementsDoes this investment let you use the weapon, spell or equipment you actually want?
SurvivabilityDo you have enough margin to survive mistakes and continue learning fights?
ResourcesDoes the build have enough stamina or casting capacity to perform its intended loop?
Damage scalingAre additional points meaningfully improving the attack plan you use most?

Do not invest without a job

A focused build gives each stat a reason to exist. Avoid spreading points simply because every number looks useful.
