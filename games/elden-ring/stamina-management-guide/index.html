Elden Ring Stamina Management Guide

A practical Elden Ring stamina management guide for attacking, defending, repositioning and keeping enough reserve to recover from mistakes.

Treat stamina as an action budget

Every attack, dodge, block or sprint competes for the same short-term resource. Good stamina use keeps at least one safe response available.

Do not spend to zero without a reason

Emptying the bar may maximize one damage window but can remove your ability to react to the next attack.

Recovery windows matter

Learn when the enemy gives you enough time to recover stamina instead of filling every opening with another attack.

Heavy setups need more planning

Slower attacks and heavier defensive actions can consume larger chunks of your action budget, making timing more important.
