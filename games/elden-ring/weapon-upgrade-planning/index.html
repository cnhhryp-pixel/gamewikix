Elden Ring Weapon Upgrade Planning Guide

Plan Elden Ring weapon upgrades by focusing resources on setups you actually use and keeping a practical backup option.

Upgrade around a real plan

Investment is most efficient when it supports a weapon you already understand and intend to keep using.

Keep one main option

A focused main weapon is easier to maintain than several partially upgraded experiments.

Maintain a backup for bad matchups

A second option can solve range, damage-type or status problems without replacing the whole build.

Test before committing heavily

Moveset comfort and stamina demands matter as much as theoretical damage.
