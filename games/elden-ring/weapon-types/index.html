Elden Ring Weapon Types Overview

An Elden Ring weapon type overview focused on reach, speed, moveset feel, stamina use and build fit.

Weapon class changes playstyle

Weapon choice affects more than damage. Reach, attack speed, recovery time, stamina use and moveset shape how aggressively or safely you can fight.

Weapon traitWhat it changes
ReachHow close you need to be before committing to an attack.
SpeedHow easily attacks fit into short openings.
Stagger pressureHow strongly the weapon can interrupt or pressure enemies.
MovesetThe angles and situations the weapon handles comfortably.

Test the moveset first

A weapon can look strong on paper and still feel wrong for your timing. Comfort with the moveset matters in real encounters.
