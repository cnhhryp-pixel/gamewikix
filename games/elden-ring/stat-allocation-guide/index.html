Elden Ring Stat Allocation Guide

A practical Elden Ring stat allocation framework for meeting requirements, supporting survivability and keeping a build focused.

Allocate with a purpose

Every stat investment should solve a problem: meet a requirement, improve survivability, support your main damage plan or enable a utility option.

Meet equipment requirements first

If a weapon, spell or tool defines the build, make sure you can use it before investing heavily elsewhere.

Do not neglect survivability

A damage-focused plan still needs enough margin for mistakes. Survivability is part of consistent damage because it keeps attempts alive.

Avoid unfocused spreading

Putting small amounts into many unrelated attributes can delay the point where your main setup feels strong. Keep secondary investments tied to specific benefits.

Review after equipment changes

Changing the centerpiece of a build can change stat priorities. Re-evaluate instead of assuming the old distribution is still optimal for your new plan.
