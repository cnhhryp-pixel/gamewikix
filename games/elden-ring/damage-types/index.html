Elden Ring Damage Types Overview

An Elden Ring damage type overview for understanding why one attack plan may perform differently against different enemies.

Damage type affects matchup quality

Enemies do not respond identically to every source of damage. A flexible build benefits from understanding when its usual attack plan is being resisted.

Physical and non-physical approaches

Different weapons, skills and spells can emphasize different damage categories. The practical lesson is to recognize when a matchup is unusually poor and keep a backup option.

Mixed damage has trade-offs

Splitting damage across categories can create flexibility but may also interact with multiple defenses. Evaluate performance in actual encounters rather than assuming a larger displayed total always means better results.

Use the simplest adjustment first

Before rebuilding an entire character, try a different weapon option, skill, spell or tactic that changes the type of pressure you apply.
