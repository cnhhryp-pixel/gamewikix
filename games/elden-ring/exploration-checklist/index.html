Elden Ring Exploration Checklist

Use an Elden Ring exploration checklist for routes, resources, map awareness and safe return planning.

Explore with one purpose

Decide whether you are scouting, collecting upgrade materials, searching for equipment or clearing a route.

Check recovery resources

Long exploration becomes less useful when you are forced to stop because basic recovery is low.

Mark unresolved points

Use map markers or notes for paths, encounters or areas worth returning to.

Bank progress before overextending

If you have already gained something important, consider securing that progress before pushing into another unknown area.
